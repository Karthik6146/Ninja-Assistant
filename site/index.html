What it does

An assistant that does things, not just talks.

Jarvis is a calm British butler with a dry wit. Ask in any mix of languages and he picks the right tool, runs it, and shows you the receipt.

Voice first

Say "Hey Jarvis" from across the room. Speak naturally, interrupt him mid-sentence, and hear him answer in a natural voice.

"Hey Jarvis, next song"

English, Hindi, Hinglish

Mix languages in one sentence the way you actually talk. He replies in the language you choose and switches when you ask.

"Jaipur ka mausam kaisa hai?"

Controls your PC

Opens any app or Settings page, changes the volume, plays, pauses and skips media, checks CPU and memory.

"VLC kholo, volume badhao"

Researches with sources

Searches the web, reads up to three pages and answers with the links, so you can check what he says.

"Best microphones under ₹5000"

Finds your files

Looks through Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Videos, Pictures and Music, and opens the one you meant.

"Find my Valorant clip"

Gmail and WhatsApp

Reads your new emails and WhatsApp Desktop notifications aloud. Read-only: he never sends anything for you.

"Koi WhatsApp message aaya?"

Reminders and memory

Sets reminders, keeps a Today list and remembers your preferences between conversations.

"Remind me tomorrow at 7 PM"

Asks before risky actions

Anything risky waits for your Allow once or Deny. After reading web pages or email, every action needs your click, so strangers' text can't steer him.

Allow once · Deny

Private by design

Everything happens inside your PC.

Speech recognition, the language model and the voice all run on your own hardware. There is no Ninja server, no account and nothing to log in to.

  • Works offline for chat and PC control. The internet is used only for web research, weather and downloads.
  • Your history stays on disk, in a local database in the Ninja folder.
  • Cloud AI is off. You can add your own API key later; each run asks before sharing.

Setup in minutes

No terminal. No config files. No coding.

If you can unzip a folder, you can run your own AI assistant. Ninja checks your PC and installs everything it needs.

01

Download and unzip

Get the 0.7 MB zip from GitHub and unzip it anywhere, like your Documents folder.

02

Double-click Install Ninja

It installs Python if you need it, sets everything up and puts a Ninja shortcut on your desktop.

03

Let the wizard pick

Ninja reads your memory, graphics card and disk, then downloads the best model your PC can run.

It knows what your PC can handle.

The setup wizard rates every model Fast, Slow or Too big for your hardware and recommends the largest one that runs fast. One click downloads it, with a progress bar, and Jarvis is ready.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Ninja is open source under the MIT license, and the AI models it downloads are free too. There is no subscription and no account.

Do I need a gaming PC?

No. It runs on any Windows 10 or 11 PC. An NVIDIA graphics card with 6 GB or more makes it much faster; without one, the wizard picks a smaller model that runs on the CPU.

Does it work without the internet?

Chat, voice and PC control work offline once setup is done. Web research, weather and Gmail need the internet, as you'd expect.

Can Jarvis do something I didn't want?

Risky actions show an Allow once or Deny card first, and every action shows a receipt of what actually ran. Gmail and WhatsApp are read-only.

How much disk space does it need?

About 3 to 8 GB for the model the wizard picks, plus about 1.5 GB if you add voice. The app itself is small.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. Ninja controls Windows apps, media and settings directly.

Can I use it for my business?

The app is MIT licensed. The "Hey Jarvis" wake-word model is for personal, non-commercial use only, so turn the wake word off for commercial use.

How do I remove it?

Quit Ninja from the tray, delete its shortcuts and delete the Ninja folder. Nothing else is installed except Python and Ollama, which you can uninstall from Windows Settings.
